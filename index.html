Candle Ice

An inquest in one night.

The ice is not a floor. It is a promise the water makes to the cold, and the water has never once kept it.Rime-folk saying

A detective story told almost entirely in conversation, in the spirit of Disco Elysium. Sixteen voices live in the Examiner’s head; what you say, what you fail, and who you listen to change what everyone else says back. Two to three hours. Sound is optional and made in your browser.
